FONTLOG for Ban Guesthouse

Ban Guesthouse (ນ້ຳຕົກ) is a display font for Lao, Thai, Khmer and Latin drawn from a hand-painted
sign photographed in Laos in 2019. Calm folk lettering cut from board: even round strokes, open counters, small blunt heads, unhurried rhythm.

How it was made
- Lao key letters (ນ ້ ໍ າ ຕ ົ ກ ດ ພ) were traced from the photograph (scripts/segment.py, scripts/trace.py).
- The other letters were generated on the Noto designs with a simulated brush whose size and the font's width were
  measured from the traced letters (scripts/auto.py), then merged with the traced letters (scripts/hybrid.py).
- Generated letters are drafts to be redrawn toward the traced ones before 1.0.
